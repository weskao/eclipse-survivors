# Boss material art — QA pending

Four original 768×768 indexed-alpha raster textures, generated offline by `node scripts/bake-boss-materials.mjs`. Abyss: warped multiscale noise and swirling luminous accretion filaments. Frost: projected shaded crystal faces and cold mist. Infernal: domain-warped Voronoi lava fractures and hot embers. Seraph: curved feathers with fine barbs and golden light. All 25 attacks use the corresponding base material and their existing distinct attack accents; dark Seraph attacks use Abyss material. Rendering clips to the controller's collision geometry and uses the same attack clock, with a maximum of two material/detail image draws per visible release. Actor imagery and collision remain unchanged. PNG is a temporary committed format pending Polytool WebP conversion and real-game visual acceptance; no claim of reference-image parity or professional approval.
