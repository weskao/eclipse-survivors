## 1. Owner decisions and boundaries

- D0–D2 Power Fantasy and D3–D5 Mastery Challenge share the same engine, hero identities, art and permanent progression. Both apply all legitimately owned ranked effects and only legally attuned keystones (2 universal, 1 matching signature, 1 compatible resonance). Never normalize these two tracks by player power.
- Apex Trials is an independent standardized run, not a difficulty tier: no spend or mutation of Hall ownership/attunement, no production wallet access from unlocked DEV, same rules/seed per challenge. Competitive trials do not require account progression to grant their standardized loadout.
- Chapters are semi-open: first legitimate clear of the preceding chapter's designated Standard expedition at D1 or higher unlocks the next (proposed precise rule). Already unlocked chapters may be replayed in any compatible mode and unlocked difficulty; no relocking on loss, no forced linear replay. Existing unlocks are never removed. Chapter-specific bosses/biomes use actual runtime mapping, not guessed chapter numbers. D0 may be played freely after chapter unlock; accessibility gate alternative may be reviewed later.
- Separate chapter (where), mode (objective), difficulty (pressure), oath (optional rules), and trial (fixed rules). No hidden power scaling, no device-dependent collision/difficulty, no mandatory hero or single build. No new persistent currency. No forced online mode for ordinary play. No permanent power reward for leaderboard position.
- ES5 flag `VITE_ES5_ASCENSION` is centralized, defaults to off on shared/production branches until approved, and is removable after migration/release. Off preserves pre-ES5 game behavior except explicitly accepted independent fixes; ES4 flag remains independent. Do not enable ES4 production purchases by changing ES5 flag. Production wallet and cloud schema need explicit safety review.
- Existing architecture: Vue 3, TS, Vite, pnpm 11.22.0, Node 24, Vitest, Canvas 2D, existing PixiJS 8 VFX only. Keep authoritative `balance/*.json`, existing hit/receipt/director owners; no new engine, AI library, rendering/audio loop or dependency without evidence.
- Rules: no PR, no force push, no develop/main change or merge without explicit authorization. Use dedicated branches; ES4 inbound allowlist and retirement rules remain. Follow CLAUDE.md, existing `.claude/rules`, release version +0.0.1 with carry at 99, existing CI cancel-stale behavior. User-approved ES3 art exception does not automatically transfer to ES5.
- Every feature has observed RED → GREEN → REFACTOR, targeted tests, then `make verify` (pnpm test/typecheck/build/check-release), exact SHA CI, real browser/player-flow QA for visual/audio changes. No weakening existing tests or claiming unrun checks passed. All UI bilingual zh-TW/en, desktop/tablet/foldable/phone portrait and landscape, safe-area, keyboard/touch, reduced motion, visible start/back controls, hidden scrollbars and no accidental text selection. Use shared detail surfaces and actual source art; transparent 3× WebP, Polytool `towebp`, approved facing/atlas rules. No new SVG raster substitute unless explicitly authorized; ES4 temporary SVG exception remains ES4-only. Original cues and existing audio owner; no music interruptions on taps, phase transitions, choice overlays or result.
