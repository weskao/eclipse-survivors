# Mandatory cinematic BOSS production contract

Applies to **every** BOSS Create / Read / Update / Delete operation, including future BOSSes, boss encounters, audio, HUD, art, translations and tests. This rule complements (not replaces) `character-art-3x.md`, `ui-responsive-layout.md`, `realtime-performance.md`, and `game-balance-config.md`.

## Content and music are inseparable

1. Register every BOSS in `src/game/content/bosses/definitions.ts` with a stable `BossId`, proper localized display name, approved artwork, attack patterns, arrival/intro, phase transition and defeat cues. Never add a BOSS without real, playable music.
2. **Write an original character-specific cinematic score** in `scripts/compose-boss-ost.mjs` (`THEMES`). The theme needs a distinct motif and harmonic identity, not a copy of an existing game or film soundtrack. Author three full orchestral versions (`-p1/-p2/-p3`), identical tempo/form/duration/beat grid, progressively more urgent (low strings, horns/brass, choir, tuned percussion, drums, pulse). Do not ship plain beep/oscillator-only boss music.
3. Use the licensed sampling/rendering pipeline `scripts/render-boss-ost.sh` and `.github/workflows/es2-cinematic-boss-ost.yml` to produce **committed, local MP3 assets** at `public/audio/boss/<BossId>-p{1,2,3}.mp3`. Commit source MIDI generation code, reproducible render procedure, final assets and the SoundFont's exact copyright/license receipt. Do not stream third-party tracks at runtime or download copyrighted soundtrack recordings.
4. Use exactly **one existing audio owner** (`src/game/audio/engine.ts` / `src/game/audio/cinematic.ts`): actual living actor ID selects music, actual boss phase selects the matched, time-aligned variation. Transition with a gain fade while preserving playback offset; keep sampled audio and one fallback melody from playing together. Sampled orchestration replaces synthesized beeps in real battles; synth stays only as an explicitly tested asset-load fallback. Never start another AudioContext per BOSS or per HUD, and never silently substitute another boss's soundtrack.
5. **Warning and transition states**: final 15 gameplay seconds can use `arrival-warning.mp3` and the compact countdown HUD. Delay the warning until the game's real spawn prerequisites are met. On spawning, smoothly replace the warning with the BOSS's P1 track and play one semantic intro cue; on P2/P3, move to time-aligned layers without restarting at zero; on real defeat, fade to the normal combat soundtrack, then victory only if actually earned. Pause/level-up/blur freezes the track and warning; restart/resume and mute must be safe and never autoplay before a trusted gesture.
6. Mix for **dramatic tension without masking attacks**. Distinguish BOSS melodies and orchestration, use meaningful dynamic range, controlled drums/choir/horns, soft scene-to-scene crossfades, and preserve essential damage/telegraph/voice sound cues. Avoid relentless loudness or audio fatigue. Aim for roughly -16 LUFS with true peaks below -1 dBTP during final mastering, verify with an audio meter, and do not claim this target without measurement.
7. Device and accessibility gate: desktop, mobile portrait/landscape, foldables and tablets; 0–100 music/SFX controls, mute, iOS trusted gesture unlock, low-end CPU/memory and background interrupts. The HUD countdown and BOSS bar must not occlude life/XP, touch controls or other gameplay. Respect reduced-motion and localized `en`/`zh-TW`. Prefer MP3 (iOS-compatible) and lazy-load only the active BOSS/phase, with a strict decoded-audio cache budget.
8. TDD and evidence are mandatory. Update `scripts/compose-boss-ost.test.mjs`, `src/game/audio/engine.test.ts`, `src/game/audio/boss-score.test.ts`, `src/game/bosses/countdown.test.ts`, and relevant UI/BOSS tests. Assert every `BossId` has three real score variants, synchronized phase switching, warning countdown tied to the simulation clock, defeat restoration, pause/resume, mute, error fallback and license/art provenance. Run `pnpm test`, `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm build`, `pnpm check-release`, plus real Chromium/iOS/Android listening and visual checks. Never mark music `APPROVED` before the in-game evidence is reviewed.

**No release gate bypass:** If BOSS music, licensed samples, visual QA or expected playback tests are missing, mark the encounter `QA_PENDING` and do not claim the BOSS is ready for release.
