# ES3 game-feel and replayability contract

- Important attacks, confirmed hits, criticals, elite deaths, boss phase transitions and victories need distinct, material-appropriate visual and layered audio feedback. Preserve hero/weapon identity. Use authoritative combat events and the existing audio owner; no generic beep as final art or premature hit feedback.
- Main skills must have meaningful, readable rank breakthroughs (projectiles, radius, chain, summon, elemental interactions, duration or new behavior), not only percentage damage. Keep tunable numbers in the existing balance JSON and UI descriptions consistent with runtime. Compare seeded DPS, XP, first upgrade and late-run challenge across heroes/modes; avoid runaway proc chains or dominant builds.
- Encounters must alternate preparation, pressure peaks, reward opportunities and recovery. One authoritative ordinary spawning policy; share actor budgets with faction and boss systems. Preserve objective and boss schedules, attack tells, collision, escape paths and exact-once rewards. Never count offscreen despawn as a kill.
- Risk/reward choices must explain meaningful upside and downside in both locales; never force unavoidable untelegraphed damage. Reward animation is separate from settlement: skipping, pausing or backgrounding cannot lose or duplicate XP, loot, evolutions or permanent progression.
- Local hit-stop affects animation only. Never stop simulation time, input, cooldowns, collisions, AI or essential audio. Use game-time streak windows and reset on a new run. Initial streak rewards are presentation/records, not damage multipliers.
- Reuse existing authored 3× WebP art, renderer and caches; keep important silhouettes/telegraphs legible in every quality tier. One owner per logical effect; add PixiJS work only with measured benefit. Cull decorative work, bound particle lifetimes and counts, avoid frame allocations, duplicate painting and repeated decoding. Follow realtime-performance.md and art/audio rules.
- Support en and zh-TW, desktop, phone portrait/landscape, tablet and foldable, reduced motion/flashing, independent audio controls and safe optional haptics. Keep active hero cast audio intact across in-run overlays. Never let VFX or HUD obscure danger or controls.
- Required acceptance: observed RED → GREEN → REFACTOR, full pnpm checks, real player-path browser/device QA, actual screenshots and MP4, seeded comparable workload and measured frame-time/operations/memory; physical thermal measurements only on real devices. Keep unverified items open in TODO.md. Mocked Canvas timing is not browser FPS, and drawImage counts are not GPU draw calls. Never weaken the production-art gate.
